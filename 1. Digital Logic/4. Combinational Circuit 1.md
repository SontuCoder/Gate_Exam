**Combinational Circuit**
===

>[!NOTE]
>
> - 1. A Circit does not contain memory are called combiinational circit.
> - 2. Output of combinational Circuit depends anly on present state of inputs.

# **Designing of Combinational Circuit:**

> - 1. Find the number of inputs and outputs.
> - 2. Write the Truth Table.
> - 3. Write the Logic Expression.
> - 4. Minimize the Logic Expression.
> - 5. Hardware Implementation.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
> **For Symetric Combinations:**
> 
> A = n bits, B = n bits
> Total no of Combination = 2^(2n)
> Total no of Equal Combination = 2^n
> Total no of Unequal Combination = 2^(2n) - 2^n
> Total no of Combination A>B = (2^(2n) - 2^n)/2
> Total no of Combination A<B = (2^(2n) - 2^n)/2
